I'm a final-year PhD candidate at [ÉTS Montréal](https://www.etsmtl.ca/en). I work on **LLM agents and how to evaluate them**, and on **vision and vision-language models**. I have first-author papers at **EMNLP, ICLR, CVPR, and WACV**, 

Most recently, I was a Visiting Researcher at **ServiceNow AI Research**, where I studied self-improving web agents and contributed to a benchmark for judging long-horizon computer-use agents.
I have also spent two years as a production software engineer before starting research.

Research
======

### LLM agents and agent evaluation

Agents that learn reusable skills and memories from their own past trajectories can improve without fine-tuning, but those modules cost tokens on every task. I study when they are actually worth it, and how to evaluate agents and LLM judges so that comparisons hold up.

### Vision transformers and dense prediction

How to make vision transformers trained for classification produce better spatial features for segmentation, without changing how they are trained.

### Open-vocabulary and few-shot learning with vision-language models

Adapting models like CLIP to segment categories they never saw during training, and to new tasks from only a few labeled examples.
